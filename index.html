StreamCatcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

StreamCatcher does not collect, store, or share personal information. All watchlist data, viewing history, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

StreamCatcher uses the following third-party APIs to provide content data:

The Movie Database (TMDB) — movie and TV show metadata including titles, descriptions, and poster images.

Movie of the Night Streaming Availability API — streaming availability data including which services carry each title and expiration dates.

These services may log API requests in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Crash Reporting

StreamCatcher uses Sentry to collect anonymous crash reports and performance data to help improve the app. No personally identifiable information is included in these reports.
